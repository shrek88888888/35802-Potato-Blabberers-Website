About FTC

FIRST Tech Challenge is a robotics competition that challenges students to design, build, and program robots to compete in head-to-head matches. Teams must balance strategy, engineering excellence, and teamwork.

As rookie competitors, we bring fresh perspective, boundless energy, and a commitment to learning and growing throughout this season.
